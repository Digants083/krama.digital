# Krama Pitch Video Script

Duration: 54 seconds

Style reference: simple animated product explainer, white UI mockups, pastel feature bars, device frames, cursor/action cues, one feature beat at a time.

## Voiceover

Krama is a smart digital queue system built for clinics.

Today, clinics lose time in crowded waiting rooms, manual tokens, sudden doctor delays, billing rush, and missed revisit follow-ups.

Krama brings the whole patient flow into one simple system.

With Control Queue, clinics can call the next patient, manage walk-ins and appointments, skip or update tokens, and keep the waiting room clear.

With Time Manager, doctors can set slots, manage schedules, add delays, and automatically update expected consultation times.

The Billing section helps staff close visits faster, generate bills, and keep the patient record connected.

Krama also handles reminders, rescheduling, and revisit follow-ups, so patients do not forget and clinics do not lose repeat visits.

One clinic flow. Less waiting. Better time management. Krama.

## On-screen beats

0-5s: Krama logo and tagline.

5-13s: Problem cards: queues, manual tokens, delays, billing/revisit gaps.

13-21s: Control Queue dashboard with live tokens and call-next action.

21-29s: Time Manager with slots, delay, and updated ETA.

29-37s: Billing section with paid status and patient record update.

37-45s: Reminder and revisit notifications on phone.

45-54s: Final Krama close: digital queue, scheduling, delay management, billing, revisits.
